# 6. Critical PWA / Chrome Icon Requirements

These rules are important and should not be casually changed.

## Source

Create the master icon at:

**1024 × 1024 pixels**

Then export or derive appropriate application sizes such as:

-   512 × 512
-   192 × 192
-   128 × 128
-   64 × 64
-   48 × 48
-   32 × 32
-   16 × 16

## Composition

-   The main symbol should occupy roughly **70--80% of the canvas**.
-   Keep the central silhouette simple enough to survive downscaling.
-   Avoid tiny details that disappear at favicon size.
-   Do not rely on text inside application icons.

## Background

The user explicitly does **not** want a white circle baked into the
final PWA artwork merely to simulate a Chrome icon mask.

Preferred final application-icon treatment:

-   transparent PNG where appropriate
-   maskable icon variant for PWA manifests
-   safe composition that works when Chrome/Android applies its own mask

During concept generation, a white background may be used when necessary
to avoid unwanted colored square backgrounds, but it should not become
an unintended permanent shape in the final transparent/maskable asset.

## Do Not Use

-   red/pink square backgrounds
-   arbitrary colored rounded-square tiles unless intentionally
    designing a platform-specific variant
-   a baked-in white circular mask
-   product names inside the icon
-   unnecessary text
-   oversized empty margins
-   fragile micro-details

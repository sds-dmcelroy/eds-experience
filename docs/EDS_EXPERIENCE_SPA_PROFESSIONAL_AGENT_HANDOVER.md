## 1. Mission

You are taking over the design and implementation of the **EDS
Experience SPA**, an immersive, cinematic, scroll-driven
product/marketing experience for **EDS --- Electronic Discovery
Solution**.

The immediate objective is **NOT** to design a generic Ofni platform,
framework, reusable experience engine, or another application.

> **Build the EDS Experience SPA first.**

Reusability may be considered only after the EDS Experience is working
and visually successful.

The SPA is a marketing/product journey. It is separate from the actual
EDS product/PWA. The visitor remains on a single immersive page and
moves through the EDS story using scrolling, parallax, animation, hover
interactions, layered artwork, and transitions.

The first meaningful deliverable must be something the user can open in
a browser and experience.

## 2. Critical Working Rule

**Implementation over documentation.**

There is already substantial planning documentation. Do not spend
multiple iterations creating specifications instead of producing the
SPA.

For each substantial iteration, prioritize:

1.  working HTML/CSS/JavaScript;
2.  visible browser progress;
3.  required visual assets;
4.  animation/interaction implementation;
5.  supporting documentation only when necessary.

Do not create unrelated infrastructure. Do not decide another platform
must be built before EDS. Do not turn this into an EDS business
application.

## 5. The River of Information

The defining visual metaphor is the **River of Information**. It is a
persistent visual thread, not merely a background.

The river begins on the left in **chaos** and moves continuously toward
**clarity** on the right.

Conceptual pipeline:

> Raw documents → Ingest/Capture → OCR → AI Analysis → Classification →
> Entity Extraction → Metadata → Knowledge Graph → Search Index →
> Reporting / Dynamic Binder → Secure Collaboration

The river transitions:

> **Purple → Blue → Orange → Green**

It may contain light trails, particles, floating documents, information
fragments, glowing paths, ribbon geometry, pipeline stations, and data
nodes. It must feel like information moving through EDS rather than
decorative neon.
